# Data, services and asset attribution

Inventory reviewed for the November 2026 demonstration. The source dates below refer to the data or bundled release where known.

| Input or dependency | Origin and transformation | Terms and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packaged fictional fixtures | Two demonstration profile snapshots, illustrative cash ledgers and numerical reference scenarios under fixtures/demo. They preserve selected numeric benchmarks used by the tests. The small fixture cohort serves examples and does not estimate Swiss household averages. | [Fixture terms and scope](../fixtures/demo/README.md) |
| Challenge brief, data and starter assumptions | Stefania Stan, LauzHack 2026 AI Financial Twin challenge. The historical independent ten-year comparison uses the published numeric assumptions. The original synthetic Swiss sample supplied 1,200 profiles and 24 months of transactions. The aggregate spending defaults use employment-group medians, average irregular spending and bonus shares. Education duration and tuition use S001 medians. See the calculations in scripts/build_defaults.py and the source entry in financial_twin/defaults.json. | The challenge README, “Rights and permissions”, reserves rights and provides materials for participation and review. Public redistribution of organizer files requires separate permission. A synthetic origin alone does not grant a license. The ignored organizer directory is outside the submission. |
| Education presets | swissuniversities domestic semester fees for 2026–27, doubled for annual tuition. The Swiss Federal Statistical Office 2022 graduate cohort provides gross income benchmarks one year after graduation. Presets use 80% of gross divided by 12 as an illustrative monthly net amount. | The app stores numerical observations and links to the original tables. Degree length and net conversion are assumptions. No external tables are bundled. [University fees](https://www.swissuniversities.ch/en/themen/lehre-studium/information-on-studies/tuition-fees/tuition-fees-at-universities) · [UAS fees](https://www.swissuniversities.ch/en/themen/lehre-studium/information-on-studies/tuition-fees/tuition-fees-at-uas) · [FSO graduate data](https://statistik.admin.ch/fr/asset/32172111) |
| Housing presets | October 2026 RealAdvisor city median prices per square metre, applied to an illustrative 80 m² flat. Annual rent is divided by 12. Financing, fees and maintenance are illustrative, with city sources and notes available from the presets API. | Third-party portals retain their own terms. Links and dated numeric observations convey provenance. [Example city source](https://realadvisor.ch/fr/prix-m2-immobilier/ville-lausanne) · [Swiss property tax overview](https://www.ch.ch/en/taxes-and-finances/types-of-taxation/taxation-of-real-estate) |
| Exchange rates | Frankfurter v1 serves ECB reference rates rebased to units per CHF. Results carry the provider date and live, saved or built-in status. The built-in snapshot is labelled 2026-11-20. Converted monetary results are team calculations. | ECB reuse requires acknowledgment and observance of its statistical reuse policy. These rates omit retail spreads, fees and future currency movements. [Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev/) · [ECB statistics reuse](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html) |
| Language model and web lookup | Gemini native API by default, or the operator’s model list and compatible chat-completions URL. Model outputs are validated as assumptions. Named events may acquire source URLs, notes and lookup timestamps. | Hosted service access, retention, output use and grounded search depend on the operator’s provider and agreement. The submission distributes no model weights or API credentials. [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) |
| Typefaces | DM Sans and Newsreader, loaded through Google Fonts on page load with system fallbacks. Newsreader was designed by Production Type. Google Fonts serves versioned files. The stylesheet request does not pin a version. | Both font families use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. [DM Sans project and license](https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts) · [Newsreader project and license](https://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader) |
| Interactive graph | vis-network 9.1.9, distribution dated 2023-11-03. Unmodified minified browser bundle under web/. Copyright Almende B.V. and vis.js contributors. | Dual Apache-2.0 or MIT as stated in the preserved distribution header. [Project](https://visjs.github.io/vis-network/) · [Bundled license notice](../web/vis-network.LICENSE.txt) |
| Runtime | Python, NumPy, Psycopg 3 with the binary extra, and PostgreSQL. Docker Compose is an optional way to provide PostgreSQL. Node runs browser logic tests. | Each installed dependency retains its distribution license. [Python](https://docs.python.org/3/license.html) · [NumPy](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/license.html) · [Psycopg](https://www.psycopg.org/psycopg3/docs/index.html) · [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/about/licence/) |
