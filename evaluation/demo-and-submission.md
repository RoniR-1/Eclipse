# Judge demonstration and submission

Use the [fictional Alexia walkthrough](../README.md#rehearse-the-five-year-alexia-demo). Show the starting cash, horizon and income assumptions first. Compare the two outcomes on the same date, then show how much cash the study path needs before graduation. Let judges inspect an assumption and show the accounting or replay check. Keep the provider-independent demonstration available throughout.

## Before handoff

1. Rehearse with the submitted files and an empty database. Record the environment, expected numbers, browser path and replay result. [Verification record](verification.md)
2. Include source, fixtures and their terms, setup instructions, the worked comparison and the test evidence. Keep provider keys, database credentials, caches, downloaded personal runs and restricted challenge archives outside the package. [Attributions](attributions.md)
3. Confirm platform, exact submission deadline, individual demo length, team and eligibility rules, awards, presentation format and repository or code-access requirements with the organizers. Record the announcement or organizer response and prepare the required upload. [Published rules and open questions](../faq/submission-and-judging.md)
