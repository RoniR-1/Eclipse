Eclipse explores what-if scenarios for a fictional household. It does not forecast your future and is not financial advice. Please don’t enter real personal or confidential financial details.

How your data is used

Your story is sent to the AI provider to read your numbers. A new event sends its description and the figures it needs, and a named public place or programme, such as a university, may be looked up on the web. How long the provider keeps requests depends on its terms.

Checked AI answers are saved on this server, so the same story gives the same numbers. Figures and sources for public milestones are kept in its database and reused for 180 days. Both stay until the operator deletes them. Run files you download stay on your device until you delete them.

Goals you describe in your own words are sent to the AI provider in the same way. This browser keeps your goals, theme, display currency and whether you have seen the tree tip, until you clear them.

    ABOUT THESE RESULTS

    What the model does, and what it doesn’t

    Eclipse calculates what follows if the assumptions shown hold. It explores options for a fictional household; it does not predict a future and is not personalised financial advice.

    Model limits

    • Net income only. Amounts are after tax. Income tax, AHV/IV, pension-fund (2nd pillar) and pillar 3a contributions are not modelled separately.
    • Calculated in CHF. The currency selector only converts what is shown, using a dated exchange rate; every calculation stays in CHF.
    • Inflation. Income and living costs rise with the price index. One-off costs, loan payments and costs fixed by contract stay at their stated amounts.
    • No automatic financing. Negative cash is shown as a funding gap. The model never borrows or sells investments for you.
    • Steady returns. Investments grow at a fixed assumed rate; market swings appear only in the uncertainty range.
    • The range is not a probability. “Show range” shows how far a path moves if estimates are off by set amounts. It is a sensitivity check, not the chance of an outcome.
    • Fictional defaults. Missing values can come from the synthetic LauzHack 2026 data, whose spending barely varies between households. They may poorly represent your situation.
    • AI estimates can be wrong. The AI reads your story and suggests event figures; source badges show which numbers it estimated. Review and edit them. The calculations themselves are done by the engine, not the AI.
    • Goals. Each goal is judged on its own, up to the projection horizon; two goals do not share one pot of money.
